# Suggested service fee references

Reviewed 12 October 2026. The catalogue fees are scope-based proposals informed by public Indian provider entry prices, not verified market averages. Competitor packages, government charges and inclusions differ; no direct comparator was established for every service or every tier. Rates that already fit the proposal were retained. Final scope, professional fees and applicable taxes must be confirmed before engagement.

Public comparison references:
- IndiaFilings company-registration pricing: https://www.indiafilings.com/company-registration/pricing — advertised professional entry fee ₹2,899 + GST. Our proposed tiers cover the site's own preparation, review and consultation scope, not an identical competitor package.
- IndiaFilings trademark registration: https://www.indiafilings.com/trademark-registration — advertised professional entry fee ₹1,899 + GST; government fees are separate.
- IndiaFilings GST filing package: https://www.indiafilings.com/gst-registration/free — advertised monthly filing fee ₹998 for GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and software. This is a GST filing comparator, NOT a full bookkeeping comparator.
- IndiaFilings virtual CFO: https://www.indiafilings.com/online-ca-consultation/virtual-cfo — no comparable public fixed fee established; our CFO tiers are suggested scope-based proposals.

Do not treat LLP government registration charges as professional fees or infer a professional quote from them. Other service tiers are estimates rather than independently verified competitor matches. Statutory audit Premium retains its starting-price qualifier; recurring periods are preserved from the existing catalogue.

LUT guidance reference: https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/refund/Furnishing_of_Letter_of_Undertaking_for_Export_of_Goods_or_Services.htm — registered eligible exporters / SEZ suppliers can furnish LUT to supply without payment of integrated tax, subject to statutory conditions. SEZ zero-rating applies to authorised operations; services must satisfy legal export-of-service conditions.
